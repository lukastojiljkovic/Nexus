Culture keeps museums, theatre, concerts, cinema and music. The page has five areas: "Visits", "Places", "Programme", "Guide" and "Music".

How to use it:

1. "Visits": the "New visit" button opens "Kind" ("Museum", "Gallery", "Exhibition", "Theatre", "Opera", "Ballet", "Concert", "Cinema", "Festival", "Other"), "Title", "Place", "City", "Date", "Start time", "Rating (1-10)", "Price" and "Currency", "Notes", "With whom" and "Attach a ticket". The list is read as "List" or "Calendar".
2. "Programme": "New plan" writes down what is planned, with a date and a time. Once the date passes a plan gets its answer — "Went" makes it a visit, and "Drop the plan" takes it away.
3. "Guide" is filled from an installed dataset pack holding `art.json`, with a search and the filters "Artist", "Museum" and "Period". Without such a pack it says "No dataset pack is installed."
4. "Music": "Import audio" takes MP3, FLAC, Ogg, M4A and WAV; titles and duration are read from the file. There are also playlists ("New playlist", "Add a track", "Rename", "Delete the playlist") and the "Listening log".

The data is in the profile, on this computer; attachments are kept with the profile and sent nowhere. Settings, on the "Culture" card, holds the question about plans whose date has passed.
