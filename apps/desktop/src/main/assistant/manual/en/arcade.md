The Arcade is five quick games for a break, offline and with no account: "Minesweeper", "Blocks", "Snake", "Bricks" and "2048".

How to use it: the "Games" shelf carries five buttons, and the game you pick opens below, in place. Switching to another game ends the game in progress — these five have no saved game, so leaving a board abandons it. Results are recorded on their own, and the "Scores" table shows them per game: "Played", "Best time" or "Best score", "Streak", "Longest streak" and "Last played".

Limits: only the best figures and the number of games are kept; the games themselves are not. The page makes no sound, and its animations honour the system's reduced-motion setting.
