The Emergency card carries the facts a stranger or a medic can read when you cannot speak: blood type, allergies, conditions, medications, contacts and your doctor.

How to use it:

1. "Edit the card" fills the card's fields: name, date of birth, blood type, allergies (with the severity), conditions, medications (with the dose), organ donor, health insurance number, doctor and notes.
2. "Emergency contacts" add a person and a phone number ("Person from the address book" or a name typed by hand), and the order changes with "Move up" and "Move down".
3. "Print A6" makes a sheet for the fridge, and "Print the card on A4" makes a credit-card-sized card.
4. "Delete the card" takes the card off the page; its data stays saved in the profile.

In an emergency the number to call is 112. This manual gives no medical advice and is no substitute for a doctor — it only explains where the card is and what it holds.
