A task can repeat on a rule: every day, on weekdays, every week, every month, every year, or on a rule of your own.

How to set it:

1. Open the task's "Details" and find the "Repeats" field.
2. Pick a preset: "Does not repeat", "Every day", "On weekdays", "Every week", "Every month", "Every year" or "Custom…".
3. Under "Custom…" you pick "Frequency" ("Daily", "Weekdays", "Weekly", "Monthly", "Yearly"), "Every" and "Days"; for monthly also "Mode" ("By date" or "By weekday", with "Day of month" or "Position" and "Day").
4. "End" is "Never", "On a date" or "After N repeats".

A repeating task carries the marker "Repeats". When you finish it, the next occurrence is written and reads "Next:".

Limits: with no date there is no repeat — you read "Set a date to enable repeating." For a series, an edit or a delete always asks which occurrences it applies to ("This one only", "This and future ones", "All"); that question has no default, and "Cancel" is the way out that changes nothing. Until the rule runs out, a finished occurrence records no completion date.
