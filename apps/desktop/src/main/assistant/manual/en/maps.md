Maps is a map of Serbia that works offline: search places, drop pins and measure a distance.

How to use it:

1. The map opens once the "map-serbia" pack is installed (Settings, the "Content packs" card). Without the pack the page says the map is not installed yet and points at that card.
2. "Search places" looks a place up by name ("e.g. Belgrade, Subotica…"); picking a result jumps the map there.
3. The tools are: "Zoom in", "Zoom out", "New pin", "I am here", "Remove my position", "Measure" ("Stop measuring", "Clear the measurement"), "Copy coordinates" and "Pin list".
4. "New pin" asks for a "Title", a "Note" and a "Colour", and the coordinates are shown as decimals or as "Degrees, minutes, seconds"; the pin is placed at the centre of the map. The "Pin list" carries "Edit" and "Remove".

Pins are kept by the profile, on this computer, and survive replacing the pack. The data source is stated beside the map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, data under the ODbL. A style that asks for an address on the internet is refused, so the map is not drawn — the pack has been changed and should not be trusted.
