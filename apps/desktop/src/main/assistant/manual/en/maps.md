Maps is a map that works offline: download a territory, a continent or the whole world, then search places, drop pins and measure a distance.

How to use it:

1. With no map downloaded the page shows the outline of the world. Click a territory and a card opens: it says whether that map is downloaded, and offers "What to download" (the territory, its continent or "The whole world") and a "Detail": "Overview" (cities and main roads), "Towns and roads" or "Full detail" (streets and buildings).
2. The size is measured before anything downloads, and the button shows it: "Download (size)". One map downloads at a time; "Pause", "Resume" and "Cancel the download" are on the card. A region that is too large at a detail says so — choose less detail or a smaller region.
3. Downloading needs the "Offline + update checks + downloads" mode (Settings, "Privacy", "Network and updates"), which applies from the next launch. A downloaded map needs no network.
4. "Download maps" in the toolbar shows the outline again, with the downloaded territories tinted. A downloaded territory's card offers "Show on the map", "Remove", "Update (size)" when newer data exists, and "Replace (size)" when another detail is chosen.
5. "Search places" looks a place up by name in the downloaded maps; each result names its kind (city, village…), and picking one moves the map there.
6. The tools are: "Zoom in", "Zoom out", "New pin", "I am here", "Remove my position", "Measure" ("Stop measuring", "Clear the measurement"), "Copy coordinates" and "Pin list".
7. "New pin" asks for a "Title", a "Note" and a "Colour", and the coordinates are shown as decimals or as "Degrees, minutes, seconds"; the pin is placed at the centre of the map. The "Pin list" carries "Edit" and "Remove".

The maps are cut from the Protomaps build of OpenStreetMap and kept as files on this computer, outside the profile; the card shows the date of each map's data. Pins are kept by the profile and stay when a map is removed. The credit is shown beside the map: © OpenStreetMap contributors, Protomaps, and the land cover © ESA WorldCover; the data is under the ODbL.
