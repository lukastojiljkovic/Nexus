The "Data" category holds everything that enters or leaves your profile.

What it holds:

1. "Backup and restore" — exporting the whole profile into one archive, and restoring from an archive. The details are on the "Backup" and "Restore from an archive" pages.
2. "Import and export" — a list of eight rows: "Import from an archive", "Import a calendar (.ics)", "Calendar export", "Import from Anki (.apkg)", "Import tasks (.csv)", "Import a statement (.csv)", "Import via an AI assistant" and "Import notes (.md)".
3. "Content packs" — public content (offline Wikipedia, a map) installed from a disk or a USB stick. Nexus accepts only a pack signed with its own key, and the content stays in a folder beside the encrypted database and is not part of a profile backup.

Limits: sync is on hold and does not appear on the page in this version. Every import can be undone with one click, but only until you lock or close the app. An automatic backup never carries private notes; a manual export carries them only while the section is unlocked and only with the archive password.
