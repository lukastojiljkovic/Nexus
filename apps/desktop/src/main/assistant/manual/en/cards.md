Cards is nine games — six solitaires ("Klondike", "FreeCell", "Spider", "Pyramid", "TriPeaks" and "Golf") and three against the computer ("Hearts", "Spades" and "Tablic").

How to use it: the page has two faces. The first is the shelf with the nine games, their record and their saved games, the second is the table being played. "New game" deals from a random number; FreeCell also lets you type a game number from 1 to 32 000, as in the classic game. The rules are picked before the deal (one card at a time or three, say), and the level ("Easy", "Medium", "Hard") for the games against the computer. A card is chosen with a click and the place it can go is marked; Tab moves through the cards, Enter chooses, Escape clears the choice.

Every game is written move by move, so it continues after the page is closed. "Back" takes the last move back — and against the computer its answer too, so it is your turn again. The solitaires whose engines can say so carry "Hint" and "Play the safe move", and Pyramid and Golf also carry "Check the deal", which says whether the deal in front of you can be solved. "Stop" ends a game without a win.

Record: the "Record" table notes every finished deal, per game and rules — how many were played and won, the best score in the game's own direction (for Hearts and Golf a smaller number is better) and the fastest time. Everything stays in the profile on this computer.
