Focus is a Pomodoro timer: the phases "Work", "Break" and "Long break", today's finished phases and the last 7 days. The same timer is what "Study" uses.

How to start a phase:

1. Under "Next phase" click "Start" to accept the cycle's suggestion, or pick explicitly "Start work", "Start a break" or "Start a long break".
2. Optionally pick "What you are working on" ("Nothing", "Tasks" or "Subjects") and type a "Phase name" — the name stays with the phase even when the task is gone.
3. While the phase runs the buttons are "Pause"/"Resume" and "Finish"; "Discard" closes the phase without recording it and asks for confirmation.

"Today" totals the phases by kind ("Work", "Break", "Long break"), and "Last 7 days" holds the history with delete and undo.

Limits: the phase lengths and how many work phases go before a long break are set in Settings, on the "Focus" card; the change applies to phases you start from then on, and a finished phase keeps the time it really ran for. A phase past its plan keeps running until you finish it.
