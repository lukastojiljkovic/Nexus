The calendar holds your events and, in the same view, what the other modules write into a day: tasks, exams, study blocks, birthdays and subscriptions.

How to add an event:

1. In the form above the view, fill in "Event title" (required) and "Date" (required).
2. Times go into "From" and "To"; "All day" removes the hours, and "Location (optional)" adds a place.
3. Click "Add" to save, or "Cancel" to leave. The end time cannot be before the start time.

The view is switched with "View": "Month", "Week", "Day", "Semester", "Agenda" and "Documents". Under "Sources" you turn "Events", "Tasks", "Exams", "Study", "Birthdays" and "Subscriptions" on and off.

Event repeat: the form carries a frequency and an end. "Reminders" can fire "At the start time" or "before"; an event can be saved as a template ("Save as template").

Limits: "Semester" is anchored to the dates set in Settings (the "Calendar" card) and slides from the current month without them. Documents and people have pages of their own.
