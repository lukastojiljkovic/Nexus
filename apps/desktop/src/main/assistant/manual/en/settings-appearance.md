The "Appearance" card changes how the app looks and how it reads time. Everything here is for this device.

What is set here:

1. "Language" — the change is immediate, with no restart. The list offers the languages that are translated.
2. "Theme" — "Day", "Night" or "System". "System" follows the light or dark theme setting on this computer.
3. "Accent colour" — "Gold", "Bronze", "Olive", "Forest", "Jade", "Rose", "Burgundy", "Graphite".
4. "First day of the week" — "Monday" or "Sunday"; it changes the calendar's month grid and week view.
5. "Default event duration" — "30 minutes", "1 hour", "1 hour and 30 minutes", "2 hours"; how long a new event runs until you say otherwise.
6. "Time format" — "24-hour (14:00)" or "12-hour (2:00 PM)". The time input fields keep whatever shape the system draws.

Limits: the language also changes notifications sent by the system. The semester dates are not here — they belong to the profile and sit on the "Calendar" card, under "Modules".
