"Profile and security" holds three cards: "Profile", "Profiles" and "Security".

Card "Profile":

1. Type a "Name" and click "Save" (the name must be 1–80 characters).
2. "Profile picture" goes through "Choose a picture" and "Remove picture". The picture is cropped to a square automatically.

Card "Profiles":

1. A business profile keeps work separate from personal, under the same account and the same passcode.
2. Switching to another profile asks for the account passcode ("Switch to profile").
3. "Delete" deletes a business profile for good and asks you to type the exact profile name; the personal profile cannot be deleted.

Card "Security":

1. "Change passcode" asks for "Current passcode", "New passcode" and "Confirm new passcode".
2. "Make a new recovery code" cancels the old one immediately — if the old one is written down anywhere, write the new one over it.
3. "Auto-lock" picks how much inactivity locks Nexus ("After 5 minutes", "After 15 minutes", "After 30 minutes", "After 1 hour" or "Never").

Limits: a passcode must be at least 8 characters, with at least one letter and one digit. Without a recovery code, a forgotten passcode means permanently unreachable data.
