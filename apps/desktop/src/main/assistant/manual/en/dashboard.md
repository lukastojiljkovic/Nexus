The dashboard is the first page you see: a greeting, a short strip with what today holds, and cards (widgets) that gather tasks, events, notes and more.

How to change the layout:

1. Click "Edit" above the cards.
2. Click "Add widget", then "Add" beside the card you want. A card the board already carries reads "already added".
3. A card's menu ("⋮") holds "Move up", "Move down", "Size" ("Small", "Medium", "Large") and "Remove".
4. Click "Done" to leave edit mode.

The switcher beside the greeting picks the board. In edit mode it offers "New board…", "Rename board" and "Delete board"; the board "Home" can be neither renamed nor deleted, and removing the last card brings the default layout back.

Limits: the catalogue offers only what the modules that are on publish. You set the board's background image in Settings, on the "Dashboard" card.
