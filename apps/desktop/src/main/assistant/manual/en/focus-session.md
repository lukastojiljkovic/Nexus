A phase can be attached to a task or a subject, and that keeps the row readable in the history even when the task is gone.

How to attach and close it:

1. Under "Next phase" pick "What you are working on": "Nothing", "Tasks" or "Subjects".
2. Type a "Phase name" (Writing the report, say) — the name stays recorded with the phase.
3. Start the phase ("Start", "Start work", "Start a break" or "Start a long break").
4. While it runs, "Pause" and "Resume" move the clock, "Finish" records it, and "Discard" closes it without recording and asks for confirmation ("The phase closes without being recorded in the history — the time that passed is lost.").

What stays: "Today" totals the phases by kind ("Work", "Break", "Long break") and says "No breaks today." when there were none; "Last 7 days" holds the rows with delete and undo. A phase that ran past its plan carries "Overrun", and a phase with no plan at all (one from Study, say) carries "No plan".

Limits: an overrun phase keeps running until you finish it. A phase started in Study shows here too, because it is the same timer, and the phase lengths are set in Settings, on the "Focus" card.
