Study holds subjects, exams, decks of cards and preparation plans. Cards are reviewed on a schedule, and the same timer that measures a review is "Focus".

How to start:

1. Click "Add subject" — a name is enough. Later you add "Add exam" ("Exam type": "Written", "Oral", "Midterm"; "Exam date"; "Scope (optional)"), "Add material" and "Link a note".
2. Cards live in a deck: "Add deck", then "Add card". A card is "Basic", "Cloze" or "Problem".
3. You rate a review with "Again", "Hard", "Good" and "Easy". "Practise" runs a session over the decks you pick, and "Start focus" measures the time.
4. For an exam you make a plan: "Add plan", then topics under "Topics" ("Add topic"). The plan runs from the study start to the exam date.

Limits: how many new cards and reviews arrive each day, and the retention the plan aims at, are set in Settings, on the "Study" card; a change applies from the next review. Importing an Anki deck has its own page.
