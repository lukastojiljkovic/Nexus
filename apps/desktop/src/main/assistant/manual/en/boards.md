Board games is six games — "Reversi", "Draughts", "Nine men's morris", "Backgammon", "Four in a row" and "Ludo" — against the computer or on one machine.

How to use it: the page has two faces. The first is the lobby with the six games, their record and saved games, the second is the board being played. "New game" starts one; the turn line says whose move it is ("you", "the computer" or "player 1"). The dice games also carry "Roll the dice", "Pass the turn" and, for backgammon, the doubling cube ("Double", "Accept", "Refuse"). The "Moves" list keeps what has been played, and the board is driven with the mouse or the keyboard ("Tab through the squares, Enter to choose, Escape to back out").

Limits: every move is written as it is played, so a game continues after the page is closed. "Back" exists only against the computer and takes back your move and the computer's answer. The "Record" table notes only games against the computer, per level; the level ("Easy", "Medium", "Hard") is picked in Settings, on the "Board games" card. Everything stays in the profile on this computer.
