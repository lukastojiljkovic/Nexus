Finance keeps accounts, entries and subscriptions. An account's balance is worked out from its opening balance and the entries you log — it is never typed in by hand.

How to start:

1. In the "Accounts" rail click "New account": "Account name", "Type" ("Cash", "Current account", "Card", "Savings"), "Currency" (a three-letter code, e.g. RSD) and "Opening balance".
2. Log entries with the form: "Entry type" is "Expense", "Income" or "Transfer"; with it go "Amount", "Description", "Date", "Account" (for a transfer "From account" and "To account") and "Category".
3. Click "Add".

The page has three views: "Ledger" (the entries), "Report" (the month behind) and "Subscriptions" (what charges itself). "Totals" stands above them, per currency. You can import entries from a statement with "Import a statement (.csv)…", and a category takes a budget through "Edit".

Limits: Nexus has no exchange rate and converts nothing — a total is never made across currencies, and a statement in another currency is refused. A transfer between two of your own accounts leaves the total unchanged.
