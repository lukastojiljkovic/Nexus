Fitness has four sections, picked under "Section": "Nutrition", "Training", "Measurements" and "Body map".

How to log a day of food:

1. In "Nutrition" one day is open; walk the days with "Previous day" and "Next day" and come back with "Today".
2. A meal is one of the slots: "Breakfast", "Mid-morning snack", "Lunch", "Afternoon snack", "Dinner".
3. Click "Add food", find it in "Search foods" (egg, bread, yoghurt), type "Amount (g)" or pick a "Common measure", then click "Log".
4. The day's total stands under "Total for the day", with "Calories", "Protein", "Carbohydrates" and "Fat".

Training has its own page; a routine is a shape of workout with no date, and "Measurements" holds the body details and the measurement log.

Limits: the values are for information — taken from public food composition tables or entered by hand — and are not medical advice. The daily targets (kcal, protein, carbs, fat) are set in Settings, on the "Fitness" card; an empty field means there is no target, and zero is a target.
