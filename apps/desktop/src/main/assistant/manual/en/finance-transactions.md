Finance starts with an account: without one there is nowhere for an entry to sit, and you read "Add an account first — a transaction has to sit somewhere."

How to keep the books:

1. Under "Accounts" click "New account" and fill in "Account name", "Type" ("Cash", "Current account", "Card", "Savings"), "Currency" ("Three-letter code, e.g. RSD") and "Opening balance" (the balance on the day you add the account).
2. In the entry form pick the "Entry type": "Expense", "Income" or "Transfer". Type the "Amount" as 1234.56, the "Description", the "Date" and the "Account"; for a transfer pick "From account" and "To account".
3. Optionally pick a "Category" ("No category" is a real choice) and add a "Note".
4. Click "Add". The ledger is read as "List" or "Cards", with "Filter by category" and "Period" ("From" and "To") as filters.

An account can be edited, archived ("Archive", and "Restore from archive") or deleted; deleting an account deletes its transactions too. A bank statement is imported with "Import a statement (.csv)…".

Limits: an amount cannot be zero, and a transfer goes between two different accounts. "Totals" is the sum of the active accounts, per currency; income and expense cover the rows on screen, and transfers are not counted. Nexus has no exchange rate and never adds different currencies together.
