The developer tools are the "Code and software" pack in the "Professional tools" drawer, for programmers, sysadmins and testers. Turn it on under "Packs…" and the tools appear in the drawer.

What the pack carries (by category):

1. Numbers and bits: "Number bases", "Integer inspector", "Bitwise operations", "Data units", "Floating-point numbers", "Microscaled blocks", "RISC-V instructions".
2. Encoding: "Base64", "URL encoding", "URL parts", "Text to bytes", "Unicode inspector", "HTML entities", "Hexdump".
3. Data: "JSON tool", "YAML tool", "XML tool", "Format conversion", "JSON to types", "Identifiers", "Text comparison".
4. Text: "Markdown table", "Filler text", "Slug", "Naming form", "Line tools", "Regular expressions".
5. Colour and design: "Colour converter", "Colour palette", "Gradient", "Bézier curve", "Contrast", "Colour mixing", "Token generator".
6. Cryptography: "Password generator", "JWT", "Hash and HMAC", "AES", "RSA keys", "RSA encryption", "Digital signature".
7. Network and system: "HTTP codes", "Paths", "IP and CIDR", "Semantic versions", "QR code".
8. Time: "Time and date", "Cron expression".

How to use one: in the drawer type part of a name in "Search professional tools", pick a tool and type the input; the result stands in the "Result" field beside "Copy".

Limits: the tools work from what you type and store nothing. What a tool does not read is refused with a sentence ("This is not a form this tool reads."), never guessed.
