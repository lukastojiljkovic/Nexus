Tasks hold everything that has to get done: a title, a due date, a priority, a list and a section. The same task is read as a list, a board, cards or a calendar.

How to add a task:

1. In "New task — type and press Enter" type the title and press Enter (or click "Add").
2. For the due date, priority, list, section, tags and reminders open "Details". A closed disclosure carries the marker "Some fields are filled in".
3. The view is switched with "View": "List", "Board", "Cards" and "Calendar".
4. The views "Today", "Next 7 days", "Urgent", "Late" and "Closed" sit above the list; lists are made in the "Lists" rail ("New list", "New sublist") and sections inside a task ("New section").

Subtasks come from the entry row ("Add subtask"). A task waiting on another one is marked "Blocked" through "Add dependency". A task that repeats is described on the "Recurring tasks" page.

Limits: a task need not have a date. A reminder and a repeat both need one: without a date you read "Set a due date to enable a reminder."
