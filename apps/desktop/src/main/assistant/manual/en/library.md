The Library keeps books, films and series: what is on the wish list, what is in progress and what is finished. The page has three sections plus one title's own panel.

How to use it:

1. "Quick add": type a "Title", pick a "Type" ("Book", "Film", "Series") and click "Add". A title is enough to start with.
2. "On the shelf" holds every title, with "Search" (title, creator, tag), the filters "Type" and "Status" ("Every status", "Wanted", "In progress", "Finished", "Dropped") and the order "Sort" ("By activity", "By title", "By year", "By rating").
3. Clicking a title opens "About this title" ("Title", "Original title", "Authors, directors, creators", "Year", "Status", "Rating", "Pages read", "Pages in total", or "Season"/"Episode"). There are also "Readings and watchings" (the "Record it" button) and "Thoughts" ("Add a thought").
4. "Collections" are your own lists: "Collection name", "Description", "Create a collection", then "Show its titles" and "Move up"/"Move down". "Suggested collections" come from installed packs and are taken with the "Adopt" button; titles you already have are not duplicated.

Everything lives in the profile, on this computer; no title is sent anywhere. Limits: a title may be at most 300 characters, at most 20 tags, and a rating is a whole number from 1 to 10. A deleted title comes back through the "Restore" link beside the notice.
