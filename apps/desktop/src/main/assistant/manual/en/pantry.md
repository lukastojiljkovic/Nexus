The Pantry tracks what is in the house, what runs out and what expires. The page has four parts: "Expiring", "Stock", "Shopping list" and "Locations".

How to use it:

1. "Add an item" opens "Name", "Kind" ("Food", "Medicine", "Hygiene", "Emergency", "Other"), "Quantity", "Unit" (pcs, g, kg, ml, l, pack), "Location", "Minimum quantity", "Expiry date", "Opened on", "Use within (days after opening)", "Note", "Barcode" and "How it is taken".
2. "Expiring" shows what expires inside the window from Settings; a row also carries how much is short of the minimum. "In date", "Expired … ago" and "No date" are the states a row can carry.
3. "Stock" is the list by location, with "Show archived too", the "Decrease by one"/"Increase by one" buttons, and "Archive" for what is used up.
4. "Shopping list" fills itself: an item whose quantity falls below its minimum is on the list ("Short by …"). A line can be added by hand too ("Add to the list"), and "Bought" adds it back into stock when the line is linked to an item.
5. "Locations" are the shelves: "Fridge", "Pantry", "First-aid box" — "Add a location", "Rename", "Move up" and "Move down".

The window for "expires soon" (in days) is changed in Settings, on the "Pantry" card; the reminder arrives at most once a day. Everything stays in the profile on this computer.
