Habits track what you do regularly: day by day in the "Today" checklist, a history per habit, and a wall of every habit at once.

How to make a habit:

1. Click "New habit" and type "Name" (e.g. Water) and, if you like, "Colour".
2. Choose "Schedule": "Days" ("Days of the week" or "Every day") or "Quota" ("Times a week").
3. If the habit has a measure, type "Daily target" and "Unit" (8 glasses, say). For a habit you only tick off, leave the target empty.
4. Turn on "Remind me" and type "Reminder time" if you want the reminder to wait for you.

During the day you tick it off in "Today" ("Mark as done", and for a measured habit "Add one"/"Subtract one"). The "Habit wall" is for looking only; a day is corrected in the habit's own calendar, by clicking the day ("mark as done" / "unmark as done"). Under "All habits" are "Edit", "Archive" and "Delete".

Limits: days the schedule did not ask for, and days before the habit was created, cannot be recorded. An archived habit is no longer expected, but its history stays.
