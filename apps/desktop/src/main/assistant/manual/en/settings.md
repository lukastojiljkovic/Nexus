Settings is one page with eight categories. It opens with the "Settings" row in the sidebar, from the app menu, with the Settings shortcut (Ctrl+, by default), or with the gear on a module's page.

The eight categories, in the order the rail lists them:

1. "Profile and security" — name and picture, profiles, passcode and auto-lock.
2. "Appearance" — language, theme, accent colour, first day of the week and the clock.
3. "Keyboard" — shortcuts.
4. "Modules" — what is on, tool packs, per-module settings and notices.
5. "Notifications" — what notifies you and when.
6. "Data" — backup and restore, import and export, content packs.
7. "Privacy" — network and updates, what is stored and where, search history.
8. "About" — version, data location and licences.

"Search settings…" filters every category at once and shows the hits with a count per category; with no match you read "No results" and "Clear the search to bring back all sections."

Limits: the category you are in is remembered for the session only — coming back to Settings starts from the rail list. On a narrow window the same place is a drill-down list with one card per category.
