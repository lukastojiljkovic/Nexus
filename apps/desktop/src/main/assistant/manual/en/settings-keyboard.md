The "Keyboard" category holds the "Shortcuts" card — the only place shortcuts are changed. Beside it, "Show all shortcuts" opens the list of every key the app answers to.

How to change a shortcut:

1. Beside an action click "Change"; the field waits with "Press a new combination…", and "Esc" cancels.
2. The combination must hold Ctrl or Alt, or be an F1–F12 key. If it is taken you read "Taken: " and the name of the action that holds it.
3. "Reset" puts one shortcut back to its default, and "Reset all" puts them all back.

The actions you can change are "Command palette", "New entry in the active module (or in Tasks)", "Quick task entry — a global shortcut" (the only one that works with Nexus in the background), "Lock the app", "Lock the private notes", "Open Settings" and "Show shortcuts".

Limits: shortcuts are remembered on this device — they do not travel with the profile or with a backup. A global shortcut must hold Ctrl or Alt and use a letter, a digit or an F1–F12 key, because the OS registers a physical key; if another application holds it you read "The shortcut is taken at the system level."
