A tool pack adds one trade's tools to the "Professional tools" drawer. The "Tool packs" card sits in the "Modules" category, and the drawer offers the same choice with "Packs…".

How to turn one on:

1. On the "Tool packs" card turn on the packs you need — "Construction and design", "Power and machines", "Code and software", "Design and graphics", "Kitchen and portions", "Law and deadlines", "Accounting and payroll", "Agriculture" or "Trades and workshop", say.
2. In the drawer click "Done" when you are finished.

Every tool that works in a regulated trade carries a short notice, and the full text is on the "Notes on the professional tools" card — including for the packs you have not turned on yet.

Limits: a pack is a set of tools, not a module — it adds nothing to the sidebar and stores no data. Turning a pack off deletes nothing; the tools simply leave the list. The tools do not replace a qualified professional and do not pick a regulation for you: limits and rates are entered, and they are printed beside the result.
