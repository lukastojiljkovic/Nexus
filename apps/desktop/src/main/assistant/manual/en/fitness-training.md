Training is a section of the Fitness module: a workout log, routines and an exercise catalogue. At most one workout is open at a time.

How to log a workout:

1. Under "New workout" pick the "Day" and click "Start" from a routine or "Freestyle workout".
2. During the workout click "Add exercise" and log the sets. The workout shows "Elapsed" in minutes, "Working sets" and "Tonnage"; optionally write a "Workout note" and click "Save note".
3. Close the workout with "Finish workout", or throw away one started by mistake with "Discard workout".
4. "Recent workouts" is read over a "Range" ("30 days", "90 days", "A year"). "Open", "Resume" for a correction, "Move to this date" for a wrong day and "Delete workout" with undo are here.

A routine is a shape of workout: an order of exercises and targets, with no date. Under "Routines" click "New routine" and fill in "Name", "Note", then "Add exercise" with "Sets", "Reps from"/"to" and a target weight or assistance. The order is changed with "Move up" and "Move down".

Limits: a second workout cannot be opened while one is running ("A workout is already in progress."). Only what is logged and finished counts; warm-ups are never counted, and tonnage covers only the exercises that carry a weight.
