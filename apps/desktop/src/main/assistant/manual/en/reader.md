The Reader reads books from installed content packs: the shelf is what the page opens on, and a book opens in place, so going back to the shelf is one button ("Shelf").

How to use it:

1. The shelf holds every installed book with its "Open" and "Continue" buttons, its "Articles" count and its "Version". A book that carries a safety notice shows "Safety notice" first, with an "I understand" button.
2. Inside a book there is "Contents" (chapters and articles), "Previous" and "Next" to page through, "Bookmark this article" and "Note beside the bookmark" ("Save the note"), and "Text size" ("Small", "Medium", "Large"). "Source" shows where the article comes from.
3. "Search" looks through article titles and article text — in one book or in every book ("This book only" / "Every book"). While the index is built it says "Building the index", and a book whose index was not built says search cannot see it.
4. "Print" prints "This article", "This chapter" or "The whole book", on "Paper" or to a file. Printing is refused when an article uses markup the Reader does not render.

The books are content packs and are added in Settings, on the "Content packs" card; they are read from this computer, and nothing is downloaded until a pack is installed. The position in a book and the bookmarks are kept in the profile.
