Study cards live in a deck, and a deck lives in a subject. A card is "Basic", "Cloze" or "Problem".

How to make and review them:

1. Open a subject, click "Add deck" and type a name.
2. Click "Add card" and pick the type. A basic card has a front and a back; a cloze card takes text with blanks `{{c1::…}}` ("Add blank"); a problem card takes a solution.
3. A session starts with "Start" under "Practise", with "Decks" and "Problems only" to narrow it. If the selection holds no cards you read "No cards to practise in this selection."
4. The rating is "Again", "Hard", "Good" or "Easy" (or the keys 1–4); "Space" or "Enter" shows the answer, "U" undoes the last rating, "Esc" leaves.
5. "Start focus" measures the time; the measured minutes show in the "Plan and reality" chart and in the study log.

Limits: how many new cards and how many reviews arrive each day, and the retention the plan aims at, are set in Settings, on the "Study" card. A change applies from the next review — cards already scheduled stay as they are. When the daily cap is reached you read "The daily review limit has been reached." Cards can also come from a note or from an Anki deck.
