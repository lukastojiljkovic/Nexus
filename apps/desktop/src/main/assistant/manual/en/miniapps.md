Mini apps is nine small tools in one place. The page opens as a grid of tiles; a tile opens its tool in place, and the header carries the way back to the grid. The tool that was open last is remembered.

The tools:

1. "Metronome" — tempo, time signature and accents, with tap tempo.
2. "Dice and picks" — dice notation, a coin, a pick from a list, a shuffle and a random number.
3. "Counters" — several named counters, kept.
4. "Scoreboard" — players, rounds, scores and standings.
5. "Typing" — drills for the Serbian and the English layout, with speed and accuracy.
6. "Date calculator" — days between dates, adding days, and working days in Serbia.
7. "World clock" — the local times of picked cities and a meeting window across them.
8. "Screen light" — the window as a light: white, warm or red, with a brightness; "Escape" closes the lamp.
9. "QR reader" — reads a QR code from an image, then copies or opens what it says.

What the tools keep (counters, the scoreboard, typing, cities, the dice history) stays in the profile, on this computer; nothing goes to the network.
