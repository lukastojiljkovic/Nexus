Private is an encrypted section for notes: it opens with a credential of its own, never appears in search, on the dashboard or in automatic backups. The module is off until you turn it on in Settings.

How to set it up:

1. Open the module and choose "What unlocks this section": "The account passcode" or "A separate password".
2. With a separate password, type it (at least 8 characters, a letter and a digit) and confirm.
3. Decide about the recovery code: "Regenerate the recovery code" or "No recovery code" (with the confirmation "I understand: without the password and without the code, private notes are irrecoverable.").
4. Click "Turn on private notes".

After that you open the section with "Unlock" and close it with "Lock". A note is made with "New note"; the menu "More options" holds "Delete" and "Move to Notes". Deleting asks you to type the exact title and has no undo.

Limits: while the section is locked the content is encrypted and out of reach. The section's auto-lock and "Lock on window minimize" are set in Settings, on the "Private notes" card, which works even while the section is locked.
