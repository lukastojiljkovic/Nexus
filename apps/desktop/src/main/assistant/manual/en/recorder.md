The Recorder is a voice and camera diary: record sound or video and play it back when you need it. The page has two parts: "Recording" and "Recordings".

How to use it:

1. "Recording": pick "What to record" ("Sound" or "Camera") and a "Device" (or "Default device"), then click "Record". While it runs the buttons are "Pause"/"Resume", "Stop" and "Discard". Settings, on the "Recorder" card, turns on a three-second countdown before every recording.
2. "Recordings" are rows grouped by day: "Play" plays one, "Edit" changes its "Title", "File in the diary" with a "Date", "Tags", a "Note" and "Save", and "Delete" removes it with a notice and a "Restore" link.
3. A recording with no title is named by its date and time; the name is written later. The search looks through the title, the tags and the note, and the "All tags" filter narrows the list to one tag.

Nexus asks for the microphone or the camera only while you record; if permission is refused, the page says so and points at the Windows privacy settings. Recordings live in the profile, on this computer.
