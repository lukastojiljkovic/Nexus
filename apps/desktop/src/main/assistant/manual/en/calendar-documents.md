Tracked documents live in the calendar, in the "Documents" view: passport, driving licence, vehicle registration, bank card, insurance policy, or something of your own ("Other").

How to add one:

1. Open the "Documents" view and fill in "Document type", "Document name" and "Expires".
2. Optionally add a "Note (optional)" and "Reminders" ("On the due date" or "before").
3. Click "Add".

Renewal: "Renew" opens "New deadline"; confirm with "Confirm" and leave with "Cancel renewal". "Earlier renewals" keeps the previous deadlines, until you read "This document has not been renewed yet."

The statuses are "OK", "Expiring soon" and "Expired". The "Deadlines" chart lays every document on one time axis, and a bar is drawn only where a renewal is recorded.

Limits: a document with no renewal history carries only a deadline marker, because its start of validity is not known. The same document is also read on the dashboard, on the "Expiring documents" card.
