Signals is four instruments on one page, each behind its own tab: "Morse", "ASCII", "Tuner" and "Sound meter".

How to use it:

1. "Morse": the "Text" field and the "Morse code" field convert both ways ("To Morse", "To text"). Words are separated by three spaces or a slash, letters by one space. "Tone and speed" holds "Character speed (words per minute)", "Message speed (Farnsworth)", "Tone pitch (Hz)" and "Play the tone". There is also "Decoding from the microphone", which listens to a hand key or a recording and shows "Heard", "Unit", "Speed" and "Confidence" — with the note that this is an estimate, not a measurement.
2. "Screen lamp" alternates the screen between white and black. Before it starts there is a warning that flashing light can trigger a seizure in people with photosensitive epilepsy; "Escape" stops the flashing.
3. "ASCII" is the character table, with a search ("e.g. 65, 4A, TAB, A") and a base (binary, octal, decimal, hexadecimal).
4. "Tuner" uses the microphone and the A4 reference tone. "Sound meter" shows the sound level.

The microphone opens only while a tool is started and closes when it is stopped or another tab is chosen. Nothing is stored and nothing goes to the network.
