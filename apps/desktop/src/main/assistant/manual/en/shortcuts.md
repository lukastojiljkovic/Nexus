The list of every shortcut opens with the "Keyboard shortcuts" row in the app menu or the F1 key; the same list is offered by "Show all shortcuts" on the "Shortcuts" card.

The groups on the list:

1. "Global" — the current shortcuts, exactly as they are set.
2. "Modules" — "Switch module in order": Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+9, in the same order as the sidebar.
3. "Palette" — "Move through the results" (↑↓), "Open a result or run a command" (Enter), "Close the palette" (Esc).
4. "Tasks" — "Add a subtask from the entry row" (Enter), "Cancel the entry in the row or close the question" (Esc), "Leave Select mode" (Esc).
5. "Calendar" — "Previous or next period" (←→) and "Go back to today" (Home), while the calendar grid has focus.
6. "Study" — "Show answer" (Space or Enter), the ratings 1–4 ("Again", "Hard", "Good", "Easy"), "Undo the last rating" (U), "Leave study" (Esc).
7. "Notes" — at the start of a line: "#", "##", "###" (heading), "-" and "*" (list), "1." (numbered list), ">" (quote), "```" (code block), "/" (the block commands menu), "[[" (link to another note), Ctrl+Shift+C ("Make a blank from the selected text"), Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y.
8. "Find in note" — Ctrl+F opens the bar, Enter and Shift+Enter (or F3 and Shift+F3) walk the matches, Esc closes the bar.

Limits: "Global" and "Modules" are not printed in a table but read from the live state, so a remap shows on the list at once. Shortcuts are changed on the "Shortcuts" card; F1 can be remapped to another combination like any other action.
