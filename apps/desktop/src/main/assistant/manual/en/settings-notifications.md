The "Notifications" card decides what Nexus reminds you about and when.

What is set here:

1. "How many notifications" — the "Minimal", "Normal" and "All" presets over the sources below.
2. The sources, each with its own switch: "Documents", "Exams", "Study", "Events", "Tasks", "Subscriptions" and "Habits". "Security" is always on and reads "Security notifications cannot be turned off."
3. "Quiet from" and "Quiet until" — the quiet hours. Reminders wait for the quiet hours to end; the last warning still arrives. "Clear" removes both times.
4. "Morning reminder at" — the hour of the morning notification.
5. "Default snooze" — which preset the "Snooze" button in notifications uses.

Notifications are read at the bell in the header. The list says "No new notifications." or holds rows with a source tag ("Document", "Exam", "Study", "Event", "Task", "Security", "Subscription", "Habit"), a "Snooze" button and the presets "10 min", "1 h", "Tonight", "Tomorrow morning".

Limits: "Snooze" names no duration of its own — it uses whatever preset is set at that moment. The first reminder asks once how many notifications you want; after that everything is changed here.
