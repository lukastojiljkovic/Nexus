The "Privacy" category holds two cards: "Network and updates" and "Data and privacy".

"Network and updates":

1. You pick the mode for this computer: "Offline only", "Offline + update checks" or "Offline + update checks + downloads". Each mode allows everything the one before it does.
2. Click "Save the choice". Nexus reads the mode when it starts, so the change takes effect at the next start, and "Restart Nexus" starts the app right away.
3. The mode decides what a download may reach: "Download" in "Content packs" works only in "Offline + update checks + downloads". The update check itself — "Check now", "What is new", "Download and install" — is on the "About" page.

"Data and privacy":

1. It states where the data is: "All your data — notes, tasks, events, cards and attachments — sits on this device, in an encrypted database. The key is unlocked by your passcode and is never sent anywhere."
2. "Nexus collects no telemetry or analytics. No counters, no usage reports, no profiling."
3. "Search history" — "Delete search history" removes this profile's last 20 searches. They stay in the encrypted database, do not cross into another profile and do not go into an export or a backup.

Limits: the "Offline only" mode opens no connection to the internet — not even a version check, and nothing is ever downloaded on its own.
