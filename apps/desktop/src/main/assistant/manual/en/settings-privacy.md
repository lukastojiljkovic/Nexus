The "Privacy" category holds two cards: "Network and updates" and "Data and privacy".

"Network and updates":

1. You pick the mode for this computer: "Offline only", "Offline + update checks" or "Offline + update checks + downloads". Each mode allows everything the one before it does.
2. Click "Save the choice". The change takes effect the next time Nexus starts, and "Restart Nexus" starts the app right away.
3. With the checks on, "Check now", "Download and install" and "What is new" are here too.

"Data and privacy":

1. It states where the data is: "All your data — notes, tasks, events, cards and attachments — sits on this device, in an encrypted database."
2. "Nexus collects no telemetry or analytics. No counters, no usage reports, no profiling."
3. "Search history" — "Delete search history" removes this profile's last 20 queries. They stay in the encrypted database, do not cross into another profile and do not go into an export.

Limits: the "Offline only" mode opens no connection to the internet — not even a version check. Nothing is ever downloaded on its own.
