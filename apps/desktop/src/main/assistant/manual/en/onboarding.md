Setup runs on the first start: a few questions about you, from which Nexus composes the boards, the sidebar, the accent and the modules. The screen is called "Your Nexus".

How it goes:

1. Type a "Profile name" and pick a "Theme" ("Day" or "Night").
2. "What does your week go on?" — pick at most two areas ("Work", "School and university", "Home and family", "Training and health", "Making things", "My own business").
3. "What do you do?" — write it in your own words (carpenter, lawyer) or pick what you do. Nexus recognises the work and adds tools for it, and nothing touches your data.
4. "What do you need?" and "Reminders" — which modules you want and how many notifications.
5. The screens move with "Next" and "Back", "Skip" skips a screen, and "Start" finishes.

After the first run the same questions are opened from Settings, in the "Modules" category, with the "How Nexus is set up for you" card ("Run the questionnaire again"). If you answer nothing, Nexus starts with the defaults — and the screens say so.

Limits: the answers are kept on this device; "Forget the answers" deletes them without putting the app back. A business profile has its own shorter first entry ("Business profile"), which asks for the name only.
