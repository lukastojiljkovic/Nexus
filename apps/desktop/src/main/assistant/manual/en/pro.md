Professional tools is a drawer of tools by trade. The module is off until you turn on at least one pack; an empty drawer reads "No packs are switched on" beside the button "Choose packs".

How to turn a pack on:

1. Click "Packs…" in the drawer's header (or "Choose packs" in an empty drawer).
2. Turn on the packs you need — "Code and software" for programmers, sysadmins and testers, say.
3. Click "Done". Turning a pack off deletes nothing; the tools simply leave the list.

In the drawer you find a tool through "Search professional tools" or through "Your packs", and "Recent" holds the tools you opened last.

Limits: a pack is a set of tools, not a module — it adds nothing to the sidebar and stores no data. The tools calculate sizes from the values you enter and do not replace a qualified professional; a tool that works in a regulated trade carries a notice, and the full text is in Settings, on the "Notes on the professional tools" card.
