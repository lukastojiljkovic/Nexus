Timers holds a stopwatch and named countdowns that keep running with the page closed.

How to use it:

1. "Stopwatch": "Start", "Pause"/"Resume", "Lap" and "Reset". While the page has focus, "Space" starts and pauses, "L" takes a lap, "R" resets.
2. A countdown: type a "Name" and a duration ("Hours", "Minutes", "Seconds") and click "Start". While it runs the buttons are "Pause"/"Resume", "+1 min" and "Cancel".
3. "Save as a preset" keeps a countdown under a name ("Coffee", say), and "Presets" starts it later with one click ("Start", "Rename", "Delete").

Limits: a name is required and may be at most 60 characters. A duration must be between 1 second and 24 hours. The sound at the end of a countdown is turned on in Settings, on the "Timers" card; Nexus ships no audio files of its own and uses the system's notification sound.
