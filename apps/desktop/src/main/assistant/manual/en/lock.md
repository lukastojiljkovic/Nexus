Nexus locks in three ways: with the "Lock the app" shortcut (Ctrl+L by default), with the "Lock" row in the app menu, and by itself after the inactivity you chose.

How to unlock:

1. On "Nexus is locked" type your "Passcode" and click "Unlock".
2. If you do not remember it, click "Forgot your passcode?" and continue through "Access recovery".
3. With more than one account on the computer, "Another account" leads to "Choose an account", and the sidebar row "Switch account" locks the open one.

Settings: how much inactivity passes before the app locks itself is picked in Settings, on the "Security" card ("After 5 minutes", "After 15 minutes", "After 30 minutes", "After 1 hour", "Never").

Limits: a wrong code is refused, and after too many attempts you read "Too many attempts — try again in" and a wait. Private notes lock separately: with their own shortcut ("Lock the private notes") and with the section's auto-lock.
