Board is an endless surface for drawing: diagrams, sketches and mind maps. It also carries cards that show a note, a task or an event from other pages.

How to draw:

1. In the "Boards" list pick a board, or click "New board" and type the "Board name".
2. In the "Board tools" bar pick the "Tool": "Select", "Pan", "Rectangle", "Diamond", "Ellipse", "Arrow", "Line", "Pen", "Text", "Image" or "Eraser". Each tool names its own key ("Rectangle · shortcut R").
3. Stroke colour, "Fill" and "Width" ("Thin", "Medium", "Thick") are in the same bar. Zoom is "Zoom in", "Zoom out", "Fit", or a click on the percentage ("Reset zoom to 100%").
4. "Add card" puts a note, a task or an event on the board. The card is clicked first ("Click a card to bring it forward"), then opened with "Open".
5. "Mermaid diagram" turns a Mermaid definition into shapes you keep editing.

Limits: the drawing saves itself, but a board with too many inserted images cannot be saved in one go and says so above the drawing. If the object behind a card is gone, the card stays until you remove it.
