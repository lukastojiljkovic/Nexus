Drawings opens an AutoCAD drawing: layers, measurements and print to PDF. DXF is read right away, DWG with a pack.

How to use it:

1. "Open a drawing" picks a file; until something is open the stage says "No drawing is open".
2. The toolbar holds what changes how the drawing is LOOKED AT: "Background" ("Light" or "Dark"), "Fit the drawing", "Zoom to a window" ("Cancel picking" backs out), "Cursor" and measurements.
3. The "Layers" column lists the drawing's layers, with "Show all" and "Hide all". A layer's colour is data the drawing carries, not the app's own colour.
4. "Print to PDF" writes a PDF and says where it was saved; a cancelled print and a failure each have their own sentence.

Limits: the drawing is not written to the profile — the module keeps no preference and no recent-files list. A drawing is read on this computer and never leaves it. DWG needs an installed pack; until then what DXF carries is what is read.
