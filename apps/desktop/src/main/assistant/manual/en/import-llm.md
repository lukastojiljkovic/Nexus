The AI assistant import turns ordinary text into tasks, events or cards. Nexus writes an instruction for you, you paste it with your text into a chat with your own assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini…), and paste the answer back here.

How to import:

1. Under "Import and export" open "Import via an AI assistant".
2. Pick what you are importing (tasks, events or cards) and the "Instruction language" ("Serbian" or "English").
3. Click "Copy instruction" and paste it into the chat, with your text below it. You can also read the instruction ("Show the instruction") and copy it by hand.
4. Paste the answer into "The assistant's answer" and click "Preview".
5. For cards, pick "Where the cards go": "An existing deck" or "A new deck" with a name and a subject.
6. Read the "Records in the answer", "Read" and "Importing" rows, then click "Import".

Limits: Nexus talks to no model and nothing leaves this computer on its own — you choose what to paste and where. One sentence with several blanks becomes several cards. If the profile has no subject at all, a card import stops and says the subject has to be made in Study first.
