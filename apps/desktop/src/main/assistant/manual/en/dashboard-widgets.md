Widgets are the cards on the dashboard: "Today", "Upcoming tasks", "Urgent and late", "Recent notes", "Exams", "Study", "Upcoming charges", "Habits today", "Nutrition today", "Workout", "Workout in progress", "Expiring documents", "Recent files", "Recent boards", "Recent circuits", "Focus" and "Your professional tools".

How to add and change them:

1. Click "Edit" above the cards.
2. Click "Add widget", then "Add" beside the card you want. A card the board already carries reads "already added".
3. A card's menu ("⋮") holds "Move up", "Move down", "Size" ("Small", "Medium", "Large") and "Remove". A card is also moved by dragging its title ("Drag to reorder").
4. Cards that carry a choice (the period and row count, say) show "Configure…".
5. Click "Done".

The switcher beside the greeting picks the board; in edit mode it also offers "New board…", "Rename board" and "Delete board".

Limits: the catalogue offers only what the modules that are on publish. Removing the last card brings the default layout back; the board "Home" can be neither deleted nor renamed.
