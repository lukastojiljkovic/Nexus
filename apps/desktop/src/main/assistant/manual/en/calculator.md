The Calculator works one line at a time: variables, units, history and a scientific keypad. The page has four parts: the line, the keypad, the history and the variables.

How to use it:

1. Type the sum into "Expression" — "e.g. 5 km to mi, 1/3, sin(30)". The result is computed as you type; "Enter" keeps the expression in the history, the up and down arrows recall older ones, and "Esc" clears the line. `ans` is the last result, and `#1`, `#2` and so on are results from the history, newest first.
2. "Scientific keypad" sits behind that heading: brackets, the four operations, power, square root, square, pi, factorial, percent and the decimal point.
3. "History" keeps the expressions with their results. "Search the history" filters, "Into the line" puts an expression back, "Pin" protects one from the cap ("Unpin" removes the protection), and "Clear the history" deletes everything except the pinned entries.
4. "Variables" are made in the line — `x = 5` or `f(x) = x^2 + 1`. "Forget everything" deletes the variables, the functions and the last result.

The arithmetic runs off the main thread, and a calculation that takes too long is stopped and explained. Two settings — the angle unit (degrees, radians, gradians) and the number mode (decimal, or exact big numbers) — are changed on the page and in Settings, on the "Calculator" card, and are kept with the profile.
