Importing from an archive ADDS the archive's contents — your own other copy, or somebody else's profile — alongside what the profile already has. A restore replaces; an import deletes nothing.

How to import:

1. In the "Data" category, under "Import and export", open "Import from an archive".
2. Click "Choose an archive…" and enter the "Archive password" if it is protected.
3. Click "Show a preview" and read what arrives and what is skipped.
4. If the archive carries records the profile already has, you choose between skipping them and importing anyway ("Import anyway").
5. Click "Import". An import can be undone with one click, but only until you lock or close the app.

Limits: the archive carries only what that version of Nexus knows — an unknown module or a newer record is refused with an explanation. A damaged archive, or one whose checksum does not match, is refused whole. Private notes from the archive come in only while the section is unlocked.
