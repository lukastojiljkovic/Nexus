An account is what the lock screen unlocks: the name shown on it, the passcode, and its own encrypted database. One computer can hold several accounts, and a profile lives inside an account (a business one beside a personal one, say).

How to create an account:

1. On the welcome screen click "Create account" and type an "Account name". That name is visible on the lock screen.
2. Type a "Passcode" (at least 8 characters, a letter and a digit) and repeat it in "Confirm passcode".
3. Click "Create account". All your data is encrypted with that code.
4. Write down the "Recovery kit" — it is shown only once. Click "Copy", tick "The code is written down and kept safe" and click "Continue".

After that:

1. With several accounts you first meet "Choose an account", with the states "Locked", "Needs a recovery kit" or "System vault unavailable". "Rename", "Delete" and "Add account" are here too.
2. "Delete" deletes an account and all its data for good, with no undo and no waiting period; it asks you to type the exact account name. If you want an export, do it before deleting.
3. If the passcode is forgotten you go to "Access recovery": enter the "Recovery code" and set a "New passcode".

Limits: lose both the passcode and the recovery kit and the data stays permanently out of reach — there is no back door. Data from another computer or Windows account needs a recovery kit, because the passcode from here does not apply.
