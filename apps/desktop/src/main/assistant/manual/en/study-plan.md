A study plan breaks the material into topics and spreads the days between the study start and the exam date.

How to make one:

1. Open a subject with a scheduled exam and click "Add plan".
2. Enter the daily minutes and the minutes per weekday. The daily minutes must be between 15 and 480, and each weekday a whole number from 0 to 480 with at least one above zero.
3. The study start must be before the exam date. An exam in the past, or one that already has an active plan, is refused ("The exam must be in the future.", "This exam already has an active plan.").
4. Under "Topics" add topics ("Add topic"). The order is also the priority — topics at the bottom leave the plan first — and a topic can show its own deck ("Topic deck").

As the plan runs, every block has a status: "planned", "done" or "missed". If the plan no longer fits the time left, "Suggested cut" offers the topics that would leave it ("Accept" or "Decline"); a topic that left carries "out of plan" and can come back ("Back into the plan").

Limits: a plan hangs off an exam in a subject, so the exam has to exist first. Restoring a deleted plan is refused if the exam has a new active plan in the meantime.
