Every habit has its own streak and its own history. The band above the wall totals the day: "Done today", "Active habits", "Archived" and "Longest streak running".

How to correct a day:

1. Under "All habits" open a habit ("Show details") — "Current streak" and "Longest" are there.
2. Click a day in the habit's calendar to mark it ("mark as done"), or click again to unmark it ("unmark as done").
3. The day count reads as "11/13 days" — "Of the days the schedule expects. A day still in progress does not count." For a habit with a quota the same line counts weeks.

The "Habit wall" is for looking only; a day is corrected in the habit's own calendar.

Limits: days the schedule did not ask for, and days before the habit was created, cannot be recorded. A habit with nothing behind it reads "No days recorded yet." An archived habit is no longer expected ("It is no longer expected. The history and statistics stay."), and deleting one deletes its history too.
